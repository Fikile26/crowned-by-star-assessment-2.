Who We Are

Crowned by Star is a new hair business established in 2025 in the heart of Soweto, Johannesburg.

We provide high-quality human hair products at affordable prices while helping our customers feel confident, beautiful and comfortable in their own style.

We believe that great hair is more than just an accessory. It is a way for people to express their personality, confidence and individuality.
